Why it exists

A voice that feels present — with a real agent behind it.

The best voice AI answers straight away, lets you interrupt, and sounds like someone. Most of it is also a voice with nobody home, or an agent living in somebody else's cloud. Hermes Agent by Nous Research is a real agent you run yourself — tools, long-term memory, skills, a persistent identity. Vox gives it a voice and a face without adding a second brain.

The entity is Hermes

Not a persona on top. Hermes owns the reasoning, tools, memory and context. The phone owns what has to be instant: hearing, turn-taking, interruption, the voice, the presence.

Sovereign by construction

Speech recognition and synthesis run on the phone. The only network peer is the gateway you configure. No accounts, no vendor cloud, no keys baked into the app.

One agent, many doors

Vox is one more frontend to the same Hermes that already answers you on Telegram, desktop or the CLI. Same memory, same skills — it never forks the agent.

What it does

A real conversation, on your terms.

Hands-free, interruptible

An open line gated by voice activity. Talk over a reply and it stops and listens — and your interrupting words become the next turn.

Hears you on-device

Silero VAD with Whisper (int8) or NVIDIA Parakeet-TDT via sherpa-onnx. Hermes always gets the whole utterance, checked for hallucinations, and a dim “heard” line shows what was sent.

Speaks on-device

Supertonic: ten natural voices at 44.1 kHz, about ten times faster than real time, chosen by a measured bake-off. Piper as a lighter option.

Enhanced Realtime alpha

An on-device Gemma 4 E2B “soul” on the phone's GPU takes the first beat of every turn in the agent's own voice, hears your tone, and hands over to Hermes for anything real.

Bring your own gateway

Your endpoint, your key, your network. Optional per-device scopes give each person their own memory on a shared gateway.

Built to be trusted

Keystore-encrypted key, TLS everywhere, sha256-verified resumable model downloads, local-only logs, transcript logging off by default. Privacy policy.

Questions

FAQ

What is Hermes Vox?

A free, open-source Android app that lets you talk to your own Hermes Agent by voice. The agent does the thinking, tools and memory; the phone handles listening, speaking and interruption on-device.

Do I need my own Hermes Agent?

Yes. Vox is a client, not a service. You point it at a Hermes Agent gateway you run — locally, on your own network, or on a server you control — and enter its endpoint and API key. There is nothing to sign up for.

Does my voice go to the cloud?

No. Speech recognition and text-to-speech run on the phone. The only network peer is the Hermes gateway you configure, plus the one-time model downloads you start.

Is it on Google Play?

Not yet — it's coming soon. Today, install it with Obtainium or download the signed APK from GitHub releases.

Is it finished?

No. It's a beta and a work in progress: installable and useful today, with rough edges still being worked out on real phones. Contributions are welcome.

How can I help?

Try it on your phone and open an issue with what you find, or read CONTRIBUTING.md and send a pull request.
